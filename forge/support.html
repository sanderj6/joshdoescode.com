Forge — Support

Help with Forge, our offline workout tracker

Contact us

Email support@joshdoescode.com. We reply within two business days. It helps to include your device model and the version of iOS or Android you're running.

Common questions

How do I back up my data?
Go to Settings → Back up my data. This saves a copy of your workout database — email it to yourself or save it to Files for safekeeping.

How do I restore my data on a new phone?
Install Forge on your new phone, then use the backup file you saved. There are no accounts to sign into.

How do I delete my data?
Your data lives only on your device. Delete the app to remove everything. See our privacy policy for details.

I found a bug. Where do I report it?
Email support with a short description and, if you can, a screenshot. Bug reports are genuinely appreciated.
