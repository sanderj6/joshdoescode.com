Josh Does Code

Josh Does Code LLC is a software development company based in Cincinnati, Ohio. We build web and mobile applications with .NET, Blazor, and .NET MAUI, for clients and under our own name.

About

Josh Does Code LLC was founded by Joshua Sanders, a software engineer with 14 years of experience in C# and .NET who has specialized in Blazor since its early days in 2019. Before moving into software, Josh worked in RF and wireless engineering at Charter and Verizon, where he was named on three patents.

Today the company does two things: contract development for businesses that need serious .NET work done well, and building our own consumer apps for iPhone, iPad, Android, and the web.

Services

Blazor applications
