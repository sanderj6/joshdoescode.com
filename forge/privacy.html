Forge — Privacy policy

Effective October 9, 2026

Forge is an offline workout log. Your data never leaves your device.

What we collect

Nothing. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads, no crash reporting, and no third-party trackers. The app does not connect to the internet at all.

Where your data lives

Everything you log — workouts, measurements, progress photos metadata — is stored in the app's private storage on your phone. Progress photos are saved to your device's photo library; the app keeps only the date, pose, and a reference to find them again.

Data deletion

Delete the app to delete everything. We cannot see, access, or recover your data because we never receive it.

Backups

Forge can export a copy of your workout database via the iOS share sheet (Settings → Back up my data). That file goes wherever you send it — your email, your files, your Mac. We never see it.
